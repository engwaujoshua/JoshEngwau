About me

My story

Hey, I’m Joshua 👋 I was born in Kampala, Uganda, on October 29, 2004. I grew up in what felt like the happiest family on earth, with my parents and my three sisters—Mercy, Vicky, and Promise. My parents ran Fantasy World Digital Studios, and I was obsessed with it. As a kid, I imagined growing up, running the company, and turning it into something huge—basically my own Nickelodeon. 😂 Then life changed. My parents separated, the business fell apart, and our family changed with it. I struggled with depression, loneliness, school, and finding my place. I started hanging around people I didn't really like simply because I wanted to belong. I even pretended to love hip-hop because the “cool” guys did. Looking back, I was spending a lot of time trying to become someone I wasn't. Then I met Jordan. At first, I honestly just wanted a girlfriend because everyone else had one. I wasn't serious about her, and eventually, she left. I got another chance—and messed that up too. When she finally left for good, that's when I realized something: I actually loved her. Being with Jordan had brought a version of me back to life. Losing her hurt deeply, and for a while, I lost my grip again. My grades suffered, I felt alone, and I didn't really know where I was going. But somewhere between 2023 and 2026, things started changing. A Kendrick Lamar song called Mirror became one of those unexpected turning points. The idea of “I choose me” stuck with me. So I started choosing myself. I stopped pretending to like things just to fit in. I stopped forcing friendships that didn't feel real. And I started surrounding myself with people I genuinely cared about—people like Osama, Anthony, Brian, and Omar. These aren't friendships built around looking cool. They're friendships I actually value. I also started taking better care of myself. I got into fitness, though i was very incosistent. I have started being more disciplined, and started paying attention to what I actually wanted from life. I'm still figuring things out, but I'm no longer trying to be someone else. Now I'm 21, and my friend Jom Joam and I are building a tech company. And that's what this website is really about. The journey. The things I'm learning. The mistakes I'm making. The problems we're facing. The crazy ideas. The wins. The failures. And everything I discover along the way. I don't have life figured out. I'm just building, learning, and becoming. Welcome to my journey. 🚀
